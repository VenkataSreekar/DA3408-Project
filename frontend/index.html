Codeforces practice problems picked for you: hard enough to learn from, not so hard that they frustrate.

How to use
  1. Type your Codeforces handle (the name on your profile).
  2. Choose how many problems you want and press Get problems.
  3. Wait a few seconds. You will see your weakest topics and a list of problems. Click a problem name to open it on Codeforces.

Solve chance is the model's estimate that you solve the problem. Problems shown are in the 50-70% range and focus on your weakest topics.
